# nhncd.ai site audit, 24 September 2026

Scope: the four pages of this repository (`/`, `/energy/`, `/privacy/`, `404.html`) plus `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and everything under `assets/`, as served by GitHub Pages at https://nhncd.ai (v2, live since 1 August 2026). Method: static inspection of the files, a local static server (`python -m http.server`), Lighthouse 13.5.0 in mobile emulation with simulated throttling (Edge, headless), in-browser measurements of rendered geometry and resource timing, WCAG contrast arithmetic on the palette, a full reference walk (97 hrefs, srcs and inline URLs), and live-edge checks of the deployed copy (status codes, encodings, cache headers, redirects). Three mechanical passes were delegated to Sonnet subagents (links and live edge, metadata inventory, accessibility statics); every judgement and every fix is the lead session's.

Rule of the exercise: nothing that changes visible content, a published value, a claim, a client or a logo was touched. Fixes that are content-neutral are in the pull request that carries this file; everything else is listed as a proposal for LC.

Severity scale: high (a user cannot complete a task or a page fails a standard), medium (measurable cost to users or a standard failure on one element), low (hygiene), info (measured, no action).

## 1. Summary

The site is in good shape. Before this audit Lighthouse scored 99, 90, 97 and 99 for performance on `/`, `/energy/`, `/privacy/` and `404.html`, 100 for accessibility on three pages, 100 for best practices everywhere, and 100 for SEO on the three indexable pages. No broken link, missing asset or dangling anchor exists. The deployed edge serves gzip on text, redirects `www` and `http` correctly, and every internal reference resolves.

What the audit found and fixed, all content-neutral:

**P1, medium, fixed.** `privacy/index.html` lines 23 to 25 and `404.html` lines 14 to 16 carried `preconnect` hints to `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com` and a render-blocking `<link rel="stylesheet">` to the Google Fonts CSS. Lighthouse measured the stylesheet at 1,125 ms on the critical path of `/privacy/` and attributed the page's only layout shift (0.081, the first body paragraph) to the font swap. The other two pages had already moved to self-hosted subsets with `preload` and `@font-face`; the two small pages were left behind. The fix copies that block, with only the two families the 404 page uses. Privacy note: the notice on that page states that the site sets no cookies and runs no analytics; before the fix the page itself sent every visitor's request to two Google hosts. After the fix no page makes a third-party request.

**P2, medium, fixed.** `assets/founder-luca.jpg` (800 by 800, baseline JPEG, 76,419 B) is referenced at `index.html` line 916 and `energy/index.html` line 1334 and rendered at 360 px (desktop `/`), 335 px (mobile `/`) and 200 px (`/energy/`, both widths). A WebP encode at quality 80 measures 21,816 B with PSNR 42.3 dB and a mean absolute pixel difference of 1.3 on a 255 scale, which is below visual threshold. The PR wraps the image in `<picture>` with the WebP as the first source and the untouched JPEG as fallback, adds `picture{display:contents}` so the figure's flex and grid layout sees the same child it saw before, and keeps `width`, `height`, `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`. Verified in the browser: `currentSrc` resolves to the WebP, natural size 800 by 800, and the image's bounding box is identical before and after at 1024 px and 375 px on both pages (360, 335 and 200 px squares at the same offsets; the surrounding card, button and footer boxes on `/energy/` are byte-for-byte the same rectangles).

**P3, low, fixed.** PNG recompression with PIL `optimize=True`: decoded RGBA compared equal for all eleven files. `favicon_energy_512.png` drops from 4,308 to 1,848 B, `favicon_energy_180.png` from 1,254 to 458 B, `og-image.png` from 16,986 to 15,447 B.

**P4, medium, proposed.** `/energy/` fetches the whole atlas stack at page load: three vendor scripts at lines 1938 to 1940 (61 KB raw, 22 KB gzipped) followed by two `fetch` calls at lines 1984 and 1985 (127 KB raw, 41 KB gzipped), all at High priority, before the fonts have finished. The atlas section sits about 3,900 px below the top of the page on a 375 px phone, so on a slow connection the data competes with the hero and the fonts for bandwidth the user needs first. Proposal: mark the three scripts `defer` and start the two fetches from an `IntersectionObserver` on `#atlasStage` with a generous `rootMargin` (about 1,500 px), so the map is built well before it scrolls into view but never before the fold has painted. Not committed: it changes when the map appears for a user who scrolls very fast, which is a behaviour LC should approve.

**P5, low, proposed.** Lighthouse reports one forced synchronous layout of 74 ms at `energy/index.html` line 1583: `el.flow.getTotalLength()` is called immediately after `setAttribute("d", …)` on the same path (lines 1580 to 1585, the C1.4 animation setup), which forces layout during script execution. The value is only needed to size the dash array; computing it inside a `requestAnimationFrame` after first paint removes the reflow. Not committed: it is a script change to an animation LC reviewed visually.

**P6, info.** `energy/index.html` is 117.7 KB (30.7 KB gzipped) with 31 KB of inline CSS and 38 KB of inline JavaScript. Its LCP element is the hero lede paragraph; the 3.5 s simulated-mobile LCP is almost entirely the document download and parse. The repository's README states that per-page self-contained CSS is deliberate, so splitting is an architecture decision, not an audit fix.

**P7, info, the atlas on a mid-range phone.** After the data arrives the atlas script places 340 register dots plus 10 flagships with a deterministic PRNG, runs a collision relaxation of 36 iterations over about 350 points (roughly 2.2 million pair checks), snaps dots back inside their country polygons with `d3.geoContains`, then builds an SVG of 413 nodes (370 circles) whose land, border and graticule paths carry 136 KB of path data in the DOM. Under Lighthouse's 4x CPU slowdown the page's main-thread total was 2.7 s with a Total Blocking Time of 0 ms, so no single task crossed the 50 ms long-task threshold in the traced run; the largest bucket is Style and Layout (873 ms) followed by Script Evaluation (662 ms). Interaction stays responsive: region filters toggle classes on existing nodes rather than rebuilding. Verdict: the CPU cost is acceptable; the network cost in P4 is the lever.

**P8, info, the live edge.** GitHub Pages serves HTML and JSON gzipped (`/energy/` 115,400 to 30,567 B, the topology 107,761 to 39,755 B), leaves woff2 and PNG uncompressed as they should be, and sets `cache-control: max-age=600` on every path; `Last-Modified` is the last deploy time for every file. `https://www.nhncd.ai/` and `http://nhncd.ai/` both 301 to `https://nhncd.ai/`. Nothing to change and nothing configurable from the repository.

## 4. Accessibility

Lighthouse accessibility: 100 on `/`, `/energy/` and `/privacy/` before and after; `404.html` 98 before, 100 after.

**A1, medium, fixed.** `energy/index.html` line 1352, the `a.email-card` link: its `aria-label` ("Email projects@nhncd.energy, opens a pre-filled project submission draft") did not include the link's visible text ("To: projects@nhncd.energy, Subject: …"), so a voice-control user saying what they see could not activate it and screen readers announced something other than the card (WCAG 2.5.3, axe rule `label-content-name-mismatch`). The PR removes the `aria-label` and appends the same hint in a `span.sr-only` (the page already defines that class), so the accessible name is now the visible text followed by the hint.

**A2, low, fixed.** `404.html` line 45: the content sat in a bare `div`; Lighthouse `landmark-one-main` failed. The `div` is now `main`; the body's flex centring is unchanged (the element's top offset measured 283 px at 1024 px both ways).

**A4, pass, reduced motion.** `index.html` line 64 enables smooth scrolling only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; lines 494 to 495 set `animation:none` and `transition:none` on every element under `reduce`; the C1 script (line 976) renders the resolved final frame, hides Replay and Pause, and never starts the timer chain. `energy/index.html` line 65 gates smooth scrolling the same way; lines 753 to 761 disable the record reveal, the plate transitions, the flagship halo pulse (`fsPulse`, line 293) and the C1.4 scan sweep (`c4sweep`, line 603) and set `animation:none` globally; all four scripts (lines 1401, 1714, 1891, 1962) read `matchMedia` and skip the apex count-up, the record reveal, the C1.4 phase loop and the atlas number morphs, and the flagship jump uses `behavior: "auto"`. The two looping animations (C1 on `/`, C1.4 on `/energy/`) also stop when scrolled out of view (IntersectionObserver thresholds 0.35 and 0.25) and expose Pause and Replay buttons with `aria-pressed`. No gap found.

**A5, pass.** Every page has `lang="en"`, one `h1`, a `:focus-visible` outline (with ink and CTA colour overrides), and the wordmark SVGs carry `role="img"` with `aria-label`; both `<img>` elements carry alt text, `width` and `height`; the two `target="_blank"` links carry `rel="noopener noreferrer"`; no `tabindex` above zero, no `outline:none`. The atlas SVG carries a descriptive `aria-label`, its decorative groups are `aria-hidden`, and the ten flagship marks are keyboard buttons (`tabindex="0"`, `role="button"`, Enter and Space handled).

**A6, low, proposed.** Neither long page has a skip link to `main`. Adding one is a new element with visible-on-focus text, so it is left for LC (wording, for example "Skip to content").

## 5. SEO and sharing metadata

**S1, low, fixed.** No page declared `og:site_name`, `og:image:alt`, `twitter:image` or `twitter:image:alt`; all three used `summary_large_image` and relied on the Open Graph fallback for the image. Added on `/`, `/energy/` and `/privacy/` (the alt text describes the image: the NHNCD wordmark with the line "Be Enhanced by AI." on an off-white ground).

**S2, low, fixed.** `404.html` carried no meta description. Added ("Page not found on nhncd.ai."); the page keeps `noindex`, so its SEO score stays capped by the crawlability audit on purpose.

**S3, low, proposed (copy).** `<title>` lengths: `/` 26 characters ("NHNCD — Be Enhanced by AI."), `/privacy/` 15 ("Privacy — NHNCD"); `/privacy/` description 36 characters. Search results truncate at about 60 and 160; a longer title and description are copy changes for LC.

**S4, low, proposed (values).** `og:locale` is absent (a value is a decision: `en_GB` fits the British spelling used on the site); `twitter:site` needs a handle that may not exist; `sitemap.xml` has no `lastmod` (values would come from the git history of each page). All three are harmless to leave.

**S5, info.** JSON-LD `Organization` on `/` is valid and carries name, alternate name, URL, logo, email, DIFC address and the two LinkedIn `sameAs` links; `/energy/` has no structured data. A `WebPage` or the same `Organization` block on the energy page is a proposal, not a defect.

**S6, pass.** Titles, descriptions, canonicals and `og:url` are consistent and page-specific; canonicals use https with trailing slashes matching the sitemap; `theme-color` is set per palette; `robots.txt` allows all and names the sitemap; the sitemap lists exactly the three indexable pages and excludes `404.html`.

## 6. Links and 404s

97 references walked across the four pages (23 distinct internal paths, 5 in-page anchors plus the cross-page `/#audit`, 8 SVG `url(#…)` references, 5 mailto links, 5 external URLs): every internal path resolves to a file on disk, every anchor to an element id, every SVG reference to a definition. External: the two LinkedIn company pages answer 200 (no bot block encountered); the two Google Fonts hosts answered 404 on a bare request, which was expected for `preconnect` targets and is moot after P1. Live edge: all nine sampled paths 200, `www` and `http` redirect with 301. No broken link, no 404 anywhere.

## 7. Page-specific notes

**`/energy/`.** Heaviest page (399 KB transfer, 11 requests, none third-party). The atlas is the only client-side data feature on the site; its accessibility is well handled (A5) and its CPU cost is acceptable (P7); its network timing is the one improvement worth making (P4). The C1.4 animation carries the site's only forced reflow (P5). The email card fix (A1) is the page's only accessibility change. The hero lede is the LCP element on mobile.

**`/privacy/`.** Was the least consistent page: the only indexable page still on Google Fonts, the only page with a measurable layout shift, and the only one whose own behaviour (third-party font requests) sat awkwardly beside its text. After the PR it makes six requests, all first-party, paints at 0.6 s and shifts nothing. Title and description lengths are the remaining item (S3).

**`404.html`.** Now has a `main` landmark, a description, and first-party fonts; four requests, 75 KB, performance 100.

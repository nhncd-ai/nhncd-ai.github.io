The problem

You don't lack data. You lack analysis you can fully trust.

In greenfield development or acquisition due diligence, the evaluation window rarely matches the complexity of what is at stake. What reaches your investment committee is typically one configuration, one set of assumptions — produced under time pressure by advisors whose numbers don't always agree.

The decision commits capital for 25 years. The analysis behind it reflects three weeks of sequential modelling.

What we do

01

Multiple configurations, assessed in parallel.

AI processes technical inputs, commercial assumptions, and financial variables simultaneously — evaluating the scenarios your team didn't have time to model.

02

Confidence levels, not a single recommended outcome.

Every scenario mapped with explicit probability and sensitivity analysis — so you know which assumptions matter most, and what happens when they shift.

03

Independent stress-testing of third-party assumptions.

We quantify the gap between what the developer's IM, the technical advisor's report, or the independent engineer's numbers claim — and what the data supports.

04

One integrated view.

Technical, commercial, and financial dimensions in a single assessment — eliminating the disconnect between advisors who don't speak the same language.

Every assessment is stress-tested against stated assumption ranges and reviewed by the founder before it reaches you — sensitivity-ranked scenarios, not black-box output.

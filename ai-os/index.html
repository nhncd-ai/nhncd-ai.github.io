The NHNCD AI Operating System

A company that runs on its own operating system.

NHNCD runs its knowledge work on files that AI agents act on, under written rules, every day.

Start with a conversation

Four scenes: the issue, the idea, the installation, one day of it running.

The issue.

  1. Knowledge lives in people's heads.When the person is away, the company does not know what it knows.
  2. Work runs on memory and the clipboard.The same context is typed again into every tool, every session, every day.
  3. Silence is read as nothing happened.A routine that did not run looks exactly like a quiet morning.
  4. The founder carries the list alone.Deciding what matters today is the heaviest task of the day, and it repeats.

The idea

Knowledge leaves people's heads and lands in files that agents can act on.

Under written rules. A person approves anything that leaves the building or cannot be undone.

What an installation is.

It lands in order. Each step produces something the company can see.

  1. 01

    The kit

    An open-source starter extracted from the live OS, every operator-specific fact removed.

  2. 02

    The intake

    Seven questions fill the operating manual and scaffold the first file set. Re-runnable.

  3. 03

    The first routine

    The daily brief, self-delivered by email from an always-on host at a fixed local time.

  4. 04

    The first record

    A decisions-log entry, and the first meeting record from the capture pipeline.

  5. 05

    The first board

    One page per workstream, read at session open and rewritten at close.

Then the weekly loop: an audit on day seven, a level-up on day fourteen, one shipped automation a week from there.

Five parts.

Roles
A lead agent. One session per workstream, each against its own board. A Chief of Staff that integrates.
Skills
Written, versioned procedures. A short phrase triggers one. It produces an artifact, not a chat.
Routines
Skills that run on a schedule, unattended, on an always-on host. Five run today.
Connections
One registry of every system the OS can reach, how, and when it was last checked. Microsoft 365 first.
Governance
Boards, a decisions log, per-goal mandates, approval gates, and a written model-routing doctrine.

What stays human.

  • Every external output. No email, proposal, client document or public post leaves without a person's approval.
  • Every deletion. Files move to an archive. Nothing is deleted permanently by an agent.
  • Every change to shared infrastructure, every payment, every publication. A person approves, always. No mandate can configure that away.

The boundary.

  • No autonomous sends. Agents draft and prepare. Sending is a human act.
  • No client data on consumer AI accounts. Client work runs under commercial terms with a data processing addendum and no training on customer content.
  • No health data to the model under standard terms. A health-data product needs its own arrangement.
  • Where data sits is disclosed as it is. The tenant's region, the code host, the company's own machines.

One day in the OS

NHNCD runs on it. Every day.

05:00

  1. 05:05Watch sweepNew items from the sources the founder follows land in one inbox file. No AI in the polling.
  2. 05:30Resource sweepEach item is triaged. Every verdict is logged with a reason. Nothing is dropped silently.
  3. 06:30Daily briefOne email. Planner, tasks, flagged mail, three calendars and every board, grouped by project.
  4. 07:30HeartbeatFrom here, every thirty minutes. A routine that goes silent pages a phone.
  5. MorningChief of StaffThe greeting renders every board. What happened off-app is captured and reconciled.
  6. In sessionThe boardA session opens by reading its board. Every dispatch to another model names the model, and why.
  7. Per meetingThe recordDevice audio becomes a verified English record the same day. Actions fan out on approval.
  8. Per decisionThe logOne line: what was decided, why, by whom. Append only.
  9. Before it leavesThe gateNo email, proposal or post leaves the building without a person's approval.
  10. Session closeThe board, againRewritten at close. The next session starts from the file, not from memory.
  11. NightThe receiptThe overnight agent files its receipt. No work is still a receipt. Absence is failure.

Before dawnThe machine is on. The first routine is minutes away.

This is how NHNCD runs. It is what an installation gives a company.
